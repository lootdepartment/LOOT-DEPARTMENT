05 / Art direction guide

The LOOT look.
Luxury with an edge.

Keep the visual system consistent across the House while allowing each featured identity to have its own styling signature.

Colour language

Midnight black, antique gold, deep royal purple, warm ivory and selective burnt-orange accents. Gold should feel rich and dimensional, never flat yellow.

Lighting & camera

Cinematic low-key lighting, sculpted highlights, soft gold reflections, controlled shadows, editorial close-ups and confident full-body compositions.

Materials & detail

Black satin, leather, polished gold, metallic mesh, couture fabrics, engraved jewellery, architectural textures and premium accessories.

Typography

Elegant high-contrast serif headlines paired with clean modern sans-serif supporting text. Wide letter spacing for labels and campaign titles.

Composition

Strong central figures, deliberate negative space, symmetrical palace framing or bold fashion-editorial crops. Keep the LOOT identity readable.

Overall feeling

Regal, modern, confident, mysterious and aspirational. The result should feel like a luxury fashion campaign, not a costume party or generic crypto advert.
