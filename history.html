01 / Who we are

A creative house.
A collective force.

We bring distinctive fashion, lifestyle and culture-driven brands under one creative roof — without taking away what makes each one original.

LOOT DEPARTMENT is a platform for ambitious identities. We help brands shape their visual world, tell stronger stories, reach new audiences and explore opportunities created by digital culture.

Different names can share one ecosystem while keeping their own voice, aesthetic and community. The House connects the dots; each brand keeps its own signature.

05 / Art direction guide

The LOOT look.
Luxury with an edge.

A consistent visual world holds the House together. Each label can bring its own character, while the overall presentation remains unmistakably LOOT.

Colour language

Midnight black, antique gold, deep royal purple, warm ivory and selective burnt-orange accents. Gold should feel rich and dimensional, never flat yellow.

Lighting & camera

Cinematic low-key lighting, sculpted highlights, soft gold reflections, controlled shadows and confident editorial framing.

Materials & detail

Black satin, leather, polished gold, metallic mesh, couture fabrics, engraved jewellery and premium accessories.

Typography

Elegant high-contrast serif headlines paired with clean modern sans-serif supporting text and carefully spaced labels.

Composition

Strong central figures, deliberate negative space, architectural framing and bold fashion-editorial crops.

Overall feeling

Regal, modern, confident, mysterious and aspirational. Luxury campaign energy — never costume-like or generic.

Luxury is in the details.

Silhouette, texture, lighting and attitude work together. The House aesthetic is not about adding more — it is about making every detail count.
